Wisp Agents Cloud & Grid

Your own Wisp Agents in the cloud

Wisp Agents Cloud is your own instance on a server, yours alone. Your agents keep working when your laptop sleeps, and you reach it, and your own computers, from any browser or your phone.

Sign in to Wisp Agents Cloud How it works

What Wisp Agents Cloud includes

Always on

Tasks run on your cloud instance while your computers are off. It sleeps when idle and wakes when you or a trigger need it.

Your computers too

wisp computer on puts a computer on the grid. Open it at your grid address from anywhere: no SSH, no open ports.

Your sign-ins

Sign in to Claude Code, Codex and the others on your cloud instance, with your own plans. Secrets are encrypted.

Share

Share a task, a setup or a crew member with a link, with the access you choose.

Run your own Grid

The same service on your own server for your team: wisp grid serve, with sign-in through GitHub or Google. How.

From your phone

Open your grid address on your phone and add it to your Home Screen. The phone apps are on the waitlist.

Get started

Wisp Agents Cloud
  1. Settings → Machines → Reach this computer from anywhere → Sign in and add it, or Home’s tip → Set up.
  2. Continue with GitHub or Google.
  3. Your cloud instance and your computers show under Machines.
  1. Open the Computers tab.
  2. Continue with GitHub or Continue with Google.
  3. Your cloud instance and the computers on it show there.
  1. g m opens the machines view.
  2. Send /grid-client signin --add-computer from the reply box.
wisp cloud signin
wisp cloud status
wisp computer on   # reach this computer through the grid
wisp "nightly dependency check" --on cloud
