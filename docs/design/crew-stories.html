Crew on phones as stories

Today the phone's Tasks list ends with a Crew section (one row per member, then their jobs), which pushes it below the fold and repeats what's above. These directions move the crew into one row of avatars under the top bar, like stories: the ring tells you at a glance who is working, who has news and who waits on you; tapping opens what that member is doing, full screen. Phones only: the browser and desktop keep v1. Every v1 crew feature stays reachable (Org, add a member, each member's page, their jobs, asks, check-ins). Sample data: 6 members.

A · Stories row

The Crew section leaves the list. A row of rings sits under the top bar: green, moving = working, blue = a report you haven't read, red = waiting on you (an ask or an approval), grey = idle. Members waiting on you come first, then new reports, then working, then idle. Org and + are the first and last bubbles. Tap a ring: their story, full screen.

Tasks with the stories row. The list starts right under it: no Crew section at the bottom.

Dex's story: what waits on you first, with the task page's own buttons (Approve / Deny / Open task). Tap right or → for the next member, left or ← for the previous, swipe down or × to close.

Ada's story: working now (live line), her last report, her next check-in. Long-press pauses; the progress bars show where you are in the crew.

For

Smallest change; one glance tells you who needs you; the list gets its space back; a familiar gesture with visible arrows and ×.

Against

An ask is one tap further away than today (the ring tells you, the story shows it); 6+ members scroll sideways.

B · Stories + Waiting for you

Same rings, plus a Waiting for you strip right under them when any member has an ask or approval open: one line each with its answer buttons, so the most urgent thing never needs a tap to find. The strip disappears when nothing waits. Stories open as in A.

Two asks wait: answer from the list, or tap the line for the full story.

The story is the same as A's.

Nothing waits: only the rings, as in A.

For

Asks stay one tap from the list (as today's Needs you); stories stay for the rest. Best for crews that ask a lot.

Against

Two places show the same ask (strip and story); a bit more height when asks pile up (capped at 2 lines + "N more").

C · Stories you can reply to

Rings as in A; the story gets a reply box at the bottom, like replying to a story: what you type goes to that member as a follow-up on their current job (or starts a job when they're idle). Quick reactions (👍 Good, ↻ Try again) send a one-word follow-up.

The list is the same as A's.

Ada's story with a reply box: "Message Ada…" goes to her running job; reactions under her report.

An idle member: the reply box starts a job for them.

For

Talk to a member without opening the task; fast reactions to reports.

Against

A second composer besides the task page's (one choice = one control: it must be the same composer, smaller); easy to send to the wrong member while swiping.
Recommendation: B. It keeps the one thing today's list does well (asks are answerable right there) and moves everything else into stories. A is the leanest if your crew rarely asks. C's reply box can come later inside B's story without a redesign.
