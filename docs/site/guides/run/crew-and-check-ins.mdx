A **crew member** is an agent that stays. It has a name, a role, a brief it follows on every job, a kit of skills,
and a notebook it keeps between jobs. You give it **jobs**; it can also **check in** on its own, on a schedule, and
wake up when something happens. Each job is a task you can open, follow up and ship.

## Add a member

<Tabs syncKey="way">
<TabItem label="Desktop app">
Press **Add a crew member** (the **+** in the crew rail, on the far left). Give them a **Name**, a **Role**, and
under **Their job** what they look after and what they should never do. Pick the **Harness**, the **Mode**, the
**Project folder**, how often they **Check in**, and **Jobs at once**. Then **Add to your crew**.

In the browser (`wisp --browser`, or your cloud wisp) it's the same window, menus and keys.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Phone app">
Open the **Crew** tab and tap **Add**. Give them a **Name**, a **Role** and **Their job**, pick the harness and how
often they check in, then **Add to your crew**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="TUI">
Press **g** then **c** for the crew view, then **n** for a new member.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="CLI">
```sh
wisp crew create --name Ada --role reviewer --agent codex --mode plan \
  --brief "Review every PR the crew opens. Never merge." --cron "every weekday at 9"
wisp crew                     # your crew
```
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Or declare them, and share them with the repo:

```yaml
crew:
  ada:
    role: reviewer
    agent: codex
    mode: plan
    brief: "Review every PR the crew opens. List what you would fix. Never merge."
    cron: "every weekday at 9"
    parallel: 1
```

## Give a job

<Tabs syncKey="way">
<TabItem label="Desktop app">
Click the member in the crew rail. Type in **Give Ada a job…** and press **Enter**. In any other task, write
`@ada …` in the reply box: the job is about that task, and Ada's answer lands under your message.

<Shot name="crew-member" alt="A crew member's page: the Give a job box, their jobs, their kit of skills and their notebook" />
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Phone app">
Open the **Crew** tab and tap the member. Type in **Give Ada a job…** and send. In any task, write `@ada …` in the
reply bar: the job is about that task, and Ada's answer lands under your message.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="TUI">
In the crew view (**g c**), select the member and press **a**. Or, in any task, write `@ada …` in the reply box.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="CLI">
```sh
wisp crew assign ada "update the changelog for the last release"
wisp crew ask <task-id> ada "can you check this?"   # in a task: the answer lands under your message
wisp ls --crew ada                                  # Ada's jobs
```
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Check-ins

A check-in is a job the member gives itself: it looks at what it looks after and reports, or stays quiet when
nothing is new. **Checks in** takes *Only when asked*, *Every 30 min*, *Hourly*, *Every morning*, *Weekday
mornings*, *Mondays*, *On call*, or your own words ("every 2 hours").

<Tabs syncKey="way">
<TabItem label="Desktop app">
On the member's page: **Check in now**. **Pause** stops check-ins, triggers and delegations until **Resume**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Phone app">
**Crew** tab → the member: **Check in now**, **Pause** and **Resume** are on their page.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="TUI">
In the crew view: **c** check in, **w** wake, **p** pause, **u** resume, **x** remove.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="CLI">
```sh
wisp crew checkin ada
wisp crew update ada --paused
wisp crew update ada --cron "every 2 hours"
wisp crew wake ada "the nightly build failed"   # a job because something happened
```
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Members also wake on events: **Also starts when** on their page, or `wakes:` in a formula
(`- { on: pr.opened }`). See [Automations, schedules and triggers](../schedules-and-triggers/).

## Org: who hands work to whom

A member's **delegates** are who its jobs can hand work to: other members, or agents on any harness. The **Org**
page draws them as arrows. A job that was itself delegated works alone, so work never goes round in circles.

<Tabs syncKey="way">
<TabItem label="Desktop app">
Press **Org** at the bottom of the crew rail. Select a member to choose its delegates, or drag from a card's handle
onto another member. Drag a card to move it; **Reset layout** puts everyone back.

<Shot name="crew-org" alt="The Org page: crew members as cards, with arrows to the members and agents they hand work to" />
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Phone app">
Open the **Crew** tab and tap **Org**. Tap a member to choose its delegates.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="TUI">
```text
/crew graph
```
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="CLI">
```sh
wisp crew graph                                   # who wakes whom
wisp crew update ada --team '[{"crew":"linus","why":"release notes"}]'
```
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Kit, notebook and track record

- **Skills and instructions:** an `AGENTS.md` they follow on every job, and skills they read when a job needs one.
  Drop `.md` files on their page. A member can propose additions; you accept them with **Add to their kit**.
- **Notebook:** what they remember between jobs. Edit it on their page.
- **Track record:** `wisp crew stats` shows how each member's jobs went.

## Share a crew

```sh
wisp crew export ada,linus          # a crew file (refused while it holds a secret)
wisp crew share ada,linus           # a link through wisp Cloud
wisp crew import --url <link>       # review it; members come in paused
```

## On your phone

The phone app shows your busy crew as **stories** under the Tasks bar. A ring says who waits on you, who has a new
report and who works. Tap one to see what they do now and their last report; swipe for the next member, down to
close. Reply in the box at the bottom (**Message Ada…**), or rate the report **Good** or **Try again**. See
[Phone app](../../apps/phone-app/).
