wisp runs the Antigravity CLI, `agy`, in the background, one worktree per task, on your own Google account.

## What you need

The `agy` command. The Antigravity app doesn't include it: install the CLI from
[antigravity.google](https://antigravity.google/docs/cli/headless/).

## Sign in

`agy` signs in the first time you run it.

<Tabs syncKey="way">
<TabItem label="Desktop app">
Open **Settings** (the person button in the top bar, or **⌘K** → Settings) → **Agents** → **Sign in** next to Antigravity, and finish the sign-in it opens. Or press
**Keys** and paste a Gemini key.

In the browser (`wisp --browser`, or your cloud wisp) it's the same window, menus and keys.

<Shot name="settings-agents" alt="Settings → Agents: each harness with its accounts and sign-in state, and an Add key button" />
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Phone app">
Tap the person button at the top of **Tasks** → **Settings** → **Agents** → **Sign in** next to Antigravity, and finish the
sign-in it opens. Or tap **Keys** and paste a Gemini key.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="TUI">
Send `/accounts login start antigravity default`.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="CLI">
```sh
wisp accounts login start antigravity default
wisp accounts login status antigravity default
```
Or run `agy` once yourself, or set `GEMINI_API_KEY`.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Pick it, its model and its effort

wisp reads `agy models`. Effort: `low`, `medium`, `high`.

<Tabs syncKey="way">
<TabItem label="Desktop app">
Click the harness chip in the New task footer (**N**), pick **Antigravity**, then a model and a thinking level. On a running task, the same chip in the reply box changes it for the next follow-up.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Phone app">
Tap **New task** (on iPhone, the compose button at the top right), then the harness chip under the box. Pick **Antigravity**, then a model and a thinking level. On a running task, tap the options chip in the reply bar, then the agent.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="TUI">
Send `/runtime set-agent --agent antigravity` to move the open task to Antigravity.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="CLI">
```sh
wisp "split the big component" --agent antigravity --effort medium
```
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Modes: **Plan**, **Edits**, **Full auto** (`--mode plan|edits|auto`).

## What's different with Antigravity

`agy` reads MCP servers only from its own config, and takes no instructions by flag. So wisp puts its
instructions ahead of your first message, and the agent has no wisp tools in a task: when it needs you, it ends its
answer with a question block, and you answer in the reply box.

## Open in Antigravity

<Tabs syncKey="way">
<TabItem label="Desktop app">
Open the task's **⋯** at its top right → **Open in › Open in Antigravity** (key **T**). Antigravity opens in a terminal in the side panel.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Phone app">
Open the task, then **⋯** (iPhone) or **⋮** (Android) → **This task › Open in Antigravity**. Antigravity's own screen opens in a terminal on the phone.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="TUI">
Press **o** in the open task. wisp types your prompt in for you and trusts the folder for that session.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="CLI">
```sh
wisp runtime resume-command <id>
```
</TabItem>
</Tabs>
