The wisp apps for iPhone and Android are not out yet. Until they are, wisp works in your phone's browser, and your
agents can reach your phone with notifications you answer from the lock screen.

## Join the waitlist

Leave your email at [trywisp.dev/app.html#phone](https://trywisp.dev/app.html#phone). It is only used to tell you
when the app is out.

## What the app will do

The app is built for each phone: Material design on Android, Apple's look on iPhone. Every wisp feature is in it.

- **Your computers, anywhere.** Sign in to wisp Cloud with **Continue with GitHub** or **Continue with Google**.
  Your cloud wisp and every computer you put on the Grid show up; tap one to work there. Or add a computer over
  SSH: its address and your password or key, with its host key checked by a QR code.
- **Tasks:** start one, follow it live, answer its questions, and read the report.
- **Swipe a task:** right to **Mark done**, left to **Save for later**. Each swipe also has a button. Swipe the
  task's header to go to the next or previous task.
- **Crew as stories:** a row of faces at the top of Tasks. A ring shows who is working, who has a new report and
  who is waiting on you. Tap a face to see their story, and reply to it.
- **Notifications** that let you answer or approve without opening the app.

## Until then: wisp in your phone's browser

Your phone needs a wisp it can reach. Pick one:

- **Your cloud wisp.** Sign in at [grid.trywisp.dev](https://grid.trywisp.dev). It runs on a server, so it works
  when your laptop sleeps.
- **Your own computer, on the Grid.** Turn it on below, then open your Grid address on the phone. No SSH and no
  open ports.

On the phone, open the page and add it to your home screen. It then opens full screen, like an app. On iPhone:
**Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Notifications on your phone

wisp can tell your phone when a task finishes or needs you, with buttons to answer. Set it up in avatar →
**Settings** → **Phone**. Two ways work today:

- **ntfy:** switch it on and wisp picks a long, random topic. Install the ntfy app, subscribe to the topic, and tap a
  button on a notification to answer. Answers are signed, so nobody else can send them.
- **Telegram:** make a bot with @BotFather, paste its token, and send your bot the link code. Answer with buttons or
  in your own words.

<Tabs syncKey="way">
<TabItem label="App">
Settings → **Phone**: pick ntfy or Telegram, follow **Set it up**, then **Send a test**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="TUI">
Send `/channels setup ntfy --enabled --notify asks,finished`, then `/channels test`.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="CLI">
```sh
wisp channels setup ntfy --enabled --notify asks,finished
wisp channels status            # what each channel still needs
wisp channels test --channel ntfy
```
</TabItem>
</Tabs>
