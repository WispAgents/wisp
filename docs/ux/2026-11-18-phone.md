# wisp v2 phone apps: UX proposals (2026-11-18)

Proposals only. No repo file changed, no agent run started, no setting changed.

**How I looked.** http://localhost:5180 with the Capacitor shim injected before load. iOS ran in WebKit and Android in Chrome (playwright-core). Sizes: 390×844 and 360×780, touch, light and dark. Screenshots of today are `ux-phone/today-*.png`. Mocks are `ux-phone/NN-*.png`: quick HTML drawings, not kit code. Each mock's yellow note says what it shows.

**The owner's direction, applied.** Fewer options showing. One More per surface. Progressive disclosure through sheets. Native gestures (long-press, swipe, sheet detents). Every feature stays reachable, only one level deeper. Android follows Material 3 and iOS follows HIG, both built from the kit and tokens.

**What the apps I compared share.**
- **Claude app, Code tab** ([docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mobile)): you pick a repository and branch, describe the task and submit. One composer. The permission mode is a single choice (Accept edits, Plan or Auto).
- **GitHub Mobile, Copilot agent** ([docs](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/use-copilot-agents/cloud-agent/use-cloud-agent-on-mobile), [changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2025-06-03-github-copilot-coding-agent-now-available-on-github-mobile)): a session starts with a short setup (environment, repository, branch, model and reasoning, custom agent), then the prompt, with "Attach" as a menu. Sessions sit in a list you can search and filter.
- **ChatGPT, Codex on mobile** ([report](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/5/openai-integrates-codex-into-chatgpt-ios-app), [composer teardown](https://ai-design-patterns.pages.dev/teardowns/chatgpt/composer), [BGR](https://bgr.com/tech/chatgpt-got-a-makeover-but-all-of-your-ai-tools-are-still-there)): tools and attachments sit behind one "+" next to the field. The bar under it is clean. Diffs are reviewed from the task.
- **Cursor agents** ([docs](https://docs.cursor.com/background-agent/web-and-mobile)): a PWA or app for starting and watching agents. Its phone UI is deliberately narrower than its desktop UI.
- **Platform rules:**
  - Sheets have medium and large detents ([HIG Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets)).
  - A context menu holds "no more than about three groups" ([HIG Context menus](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/context-menus)).
  - On mobile, a bottom sheet is the alternative to a long menu ([M3 bottom sheets](https://m3.material.io/components/bottom-sheets), [Material menus](https://m2.material.io/components/menus)).
  - Tab bars hide on pushed detail pages (`hidesBottomBarWhenPushed`, [Apple forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/711128); the [HIG on tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars)).
  - Terminal keys ride above the keyboard ([Termius](https://docs.termius.com/terminal/mobile-terminal), [Prompt](https://help.panic.com/prompt/prompt-keyboard/)).

Ranking puts impact first, then the smallest effort.

## 1. Reply box: 7 controls become +, the field, Send, and one settings chip — High · M

- **Today** ([today-ios-task.png](ux-phone/today-ios-task.png), [today-android360-task.png](ux-phone/today-android360-task.png)): under the field sit Attach, Full auto, harness, Delegate, Goal, Start later and Send. At 360 all seven are bare icons, each 30×30 px. Delegate and Goal are unlabelled glyphs that a first-time user can't tell apart.
- **Change** ([01-reply.png](ux-phone/01-reply.png), [02-options.png](ux-phone/02-options.png)):
  - One row: **＋** (attach: photo, file, camera, from an issue), the **field**, and **Send** (Stop while a run is going).
  - Above it, one chip sums up how the reply runs ("✦ Full auto · Codex ▾"). It opens a sheet: Permissions as a segmented control with its one-line description, then Agent (harness and model), Thinking, Delegate, Goal and Send later.
  - An option that is on shows as its own small chip beside the summary ("◎ until tests pass", "⏱ 20:00"). Tapping that chip edits it.
- **Who does this:** the ChatGPT "+" and tools menu, the Claude app's single permission picker, GitHub Mobile's Attach menu plus setup.
- Every feature stays one tap deeper. The first thing you see is the field.

## 2. Hide the tab bar on a task and pin the reply box to the safe area and keyboard — High · S

- **Today** ([today-ios-task.png](ux-phone/today-ios-task.png), [today-ios-shell.png](ux-phone/today-ios-shell.png)): Tasks, Crew and Stuff stay under every task, Shell and sheet. That costs 49–80 pt, and the reply box floats above the tab bar. Under the Thread sheet the tab-bar labels peek out, cut in half ([today-ios-thread.png](ux-phone/today-ios-thread.png), at the bottom edge).
- **Change:** on the task page and its sheets, hide the tab bar (iOS `hidesBottomBarWhenPushed`; on Android, no navigation bar on a detail destination). The reply box sits on the safe area and rides the keyboard. Back (the nav-bar Back, the edge swipe, Android's system Back) returns to the list.
- **Who does this:** Messages, Slack and ChatGPT conversations, GitHub Mobile's PR pages.

## 3. Side sheets: one sheet, opened from status pills under the title — High · M

- **Today:**
  - Changes is at ⋯ › Show › Changes, three taps deep ([today-ios-menu-show.png](ux-phone/today-ios-menu-show.png)). The Show submenu mixes Thread, Changes, Shell, Run app, Set goal, Add to crew and Next/Previous task.
  - Changes, PR, Shell and Preview open as a full-screen page with a 5-way segmented control and ✕ ([today-ios-changes.png](ux-phone/today-ios-changes.png)).
  - Thread opens as a real bottom sheet with a grabber ([today-ios-thread.png](ux-phone/today-ios-thread.png)), yet it is also the fifth segment. So one family has two presentations.
  - At 360, "Thread" is clipped at the edge ([today-android360-dark-changes.png](ux-phone/today-android360-dark-changes.png)).
- **Change** ([05-sheets.png](ux-phone/05-sheets.png)):
  - Under the title, a row of status pills that exist only when they mean something: `● Done`, `3 files +42 −7`, `PR #128 ✓`, `Preview`. A pill opens **one** sheet at its tab: medium detent first, so the answer stays visible, and drag to large.
  - The sheet's tabs are Changes · PR · Shell · Preview · Notes (Thread). It is the same component on both platforms: a segmented control on iOS, M3 scrollable tabs inside a modal bottom sheet on Android.
  - Ship it… sits at the foot of Changes, where you decide.
- **Who does this:** GitHub Mobile (the PR's Files changed is one tap from the PR), Codex in ChatGPT (diffs opened from the task), and HIG and M3 sheets with detents.

## 4. Touch targets: 44 pt on iOS, 48 dp on Android, everywhere in the composer — High · S

- **Today:** the New task toolbar and the reply toolbar use 30×30 px hit areas (Attach, Runs on, Mode, Compare, Delegate, More), and Send is 32–34 px. The VISIBLE dump at 390 and 360 shows `Attach files 30x30`, `Launch 32x32`. Mis-taps are easy, and the Compare "+" sits right next to the harness chevron. One tap on it added "vs Codex" at once ([today-android-newtask-vs.png](ux-phone/today-android-newtask-vs.png)).
- **Change:** the kit's phone variants give every composer control at least a 44 pt hit area (48 dp on Android). The glyph can stay small, with an invisible hit slop. Proposals 1 and 5 cut the count, so the row still fits at 360.
- **Who sets this:** the HIG (44×44 pt) and Material (48 dp). design/mobile.md already asks for 44 px in the app, so this is a regression against wisp's own rule.

## 5. New task: one primary action, a short setup list, and the keyboard up on open — High · M

- **Today** ([today-ios-newtask.png](ux-phone/today-ios-newtask.png), [today-android-newtask.png](ux-phone/today-android-newtask.png), [today-ios-newtask-more.png](ux-phone/today-ios-newtask-more.png)):
  - There are two Sends: "Send" in the nav bar and ↵ in the box.
  - The toolbar packs Attach, Runs on, Mode, harness, Compare "+", Delegate, ⋯ and ↵. Compare is reachable twice: the "+" and ⋯ › "Run on another agent too".
  - The placeholder ("drop logs or videos") is desktop wording.
  - About 70% of the sheet is empty, and the keyboard isn't up.
- **Change** ([03-newtask-ios.png](ux-phone/03-newtask-ios.png), [04-newtask-and.png](ux-phone/04-newtask-and.png)):
  - **iOS:** Cancel | New task | **Start** in the nav bar (the sheet pattern). The field is focused on open, with ＋ for attachments. Under it, a grouped list remembers your last choices: Project, Runs on, Agent (harness · mode), More (Goal, Worktree, Start later, vs, From an issue, Recipes).
  - **Android:** an M3 full-screen dialog with ✕ | New task | filled **Start**. Setup is M3 filter chips (`wisp ▾ · This computer ▾ · Claude Code ▾ · Full auto ▾`) and ⋮ holds the rest.
  - The in-box ↵ goes. A hardware or Bluetooth keyboard's Enter still sends.
- **Who does this:** GitHub Mobile "New Session" (setup, then prompt, then submit), the Claude Code tab (repository and branch, then describe, then submit), and the HIG modal-sheet confirm in the nav bar.

## 6. Task ⋯ menu: 12 rows and 3 submenus become 3 groups and 9 rows; Android gets a bottom sheet — High · S

- **Today** ([today-ios-menu.png](ux-phone/today-ios-menu.png), [today-android-menu.png](ux-phone/today-android-menu.png)): Details, Transcript, Rerun…, Open in Codex, Ship it…, Mark done, This task ›, Show ›, Keep and copy ›, Save for later, Archive and Delete, in five groups. The Show submenu repeats 3's sheets and adds Set goal, Add to crew and Next/Previous task.
- **Change** ([06-menu.png](ux-phone/06-menu.png)):
  - The rows become three groups:
    - **Act:** Ship it…, Mark done.
    - **Work:** Rerun…, Open in Codex, Keep and copy ›, This task › (Rename, Details, Transcript, Set goal, Add to crew).
    - **Put away:** Save for later, Archive, Delete.
  - Show › goes: proposal 3 covers it.
  - Next and Previous task become a swipe on the nav title or the list. They stay in This task › for VoiceOver users.
  - On Android the same groups open as an M3 modal bottom sheet instead of a 12-row dropdown.
- **Who sets this:** the HIG ("generally no more than about three groups"), and Material's bottom sheet as the alternative to a long menu on mobile.

## 7. Menus and popovers must be opaque or blurred — Med · S

- **Today:** the iOS ⋯ menu and the mode popover are translucent with no blur, so the transcript's text and the terminal block show through the items ([today-ios-menu.png](ux-phone/today-ios-menu.png), [today-ios-mode.png](ux-phone/today-ios-mode.png); "Codex is signed out" reads through "Save for later"). The mode popover also wraps "Trusted repos only." onto a fourth line.
- **Change:** on iOS, the kit's phone menu uses the system thick material (backdrop blur plus 85–90% surface) or an opaque surface. On Android, M3's opaque surface-container with elevation 2.
- **Who does this:** every native menu on both platforms.

## 8. Answer actions: show them on the latest answer only; long-press any answer for the rest — Med · M

- **Today** ([today-ios-task.png](ux-phone/today-ios-task.png)): every turn shows five 44 px icons (Agent notes, Restore, Fork, 👍, 👎), and every block has a 37 px "Comment on this" button. On a long transcript that is a lot of chrome.
- **Change** ([11-turn-actions.png](ux-phone/11-turn-actions.png)):
  - The **latest** answer shows Copy, 👍, 👎 and ⋯.
  - A **long-press** on any answer opens the iOS context menu with a lifted preview, or an M3 bottom sheet on Android. It holds Copy, Comment…, Agent notes, Fork from here, Restore to before this turn, and Good/Bad result.
  - Text selection also offers Comment.
- **Who does this:** the ChatGPT and Claude apps (actions under the last reply, long-press on a message), and the HIG context menus.

## 9. Agent picker: a pushed page in the reply sheet, with thinking levels labelled — Med · M

- **Today** ([today-ios-harness.png](ux-phone/today-ios-harness.png)): one floating popover packs three levels:
  - a horizontally scrolling row of harness cards with a scrollbar;
  - a model list with a search field (for four models);
  - "THINKING as the harness is set" over a slider of 7 unlabelled dots.
  It covers the transcript and anchors to a 30 px icon.
- **Change** ([09-agent-sheet.png](ux-phone/09-agent-sheet.png)):
  - Agent is a row in 1's sheet that pushes a page.
  - The harness is a segmented control: the three most used, then More ›.
  - The model is a checked list. Search appears only past about 8 models.
  - Thinking is a labelled segmented control: Default · Low · Med · High · Max (the kit's real levels), with one line on what it costs.
- **Who does this:** the Claude and ChatGPT model pickers (a list with a checkmark, in a sheet), and HIG sheets.

## 10. Task list rows: two lines, meta only when it differs — Med · S

- **Today** ([today-ios-list.png](ux-phone/today-ios-list.png)): each row has three lines: title, preview, then harness · model · "This computer". With one computer, "This computer" repeats on every row, and you see about 6 rows. Done rows are greyed throughout, so the list reads as disabled.
- **Change** ([07-list-ios.png](ux-phone/07-list-ios.png)):
  - Two lines: the title, then what matters now. That is "Asking: …", "Running · Codex · Studio", or the preview.
  - The harness and computer show only when they aren't the default or there is more than one computer.
  - The state dot carries the colour. Done titles stay ink, not grey.
  - About 8–9 rows fit.
- **Who does this:** Mail, Linear's list (title plus one status line), and GitHub Mobile's agent sessions list.

## 11. List header: fewer buttons; selection by long-press — Med · S

- **Today:**
  - iOS nav bar ([today-ios-list.png](ux-phone/today-ios-list.png)): "All projects ▾", the account icon, "✓ Select" and Compose. That is four trailing items beside a large "Tasks" title.
  - Android ([today-android-list.png](ux-phone/today-android-list.png)): "All projects ▾", Search, the account icon and a blue "✓ Select" text button. That button is an iOS idiom.
  - Rows already have long-press and swipe (`data-gesture="row.swipe row.long-press"`).
- **Change** ([07-list-ios.png](ux-phone/07-list-ios.png), [08-list-and.png](ux-phone/08-list-and.png)):
  - **iOS:** the large title *is* the project menu ("All projects ⌄", like Mail and Notes title menus). The avatar sits leading and Compose trailing. Select lives in a long-press row's context menu and in the title menu.
  - **Android:** an M3 top app bar (title menu, Search, avatar). A long-press on a row enters selection mode, the M3 standard. The extended FAB "New task" stays (correct M3).
- **Who sets this:** the HIG on bar items (keep few), and M3 selection by long-press.

## 12. Crew strip on Tasks: show only who is working or asking — Med · S

- **Today** ([today-ios-list.png](ux-phone/today-ios-list.png), [today-android-list.png](ux-phone/today-android-list.png)): Org, every member and Add take about 150 pt above the first task on every visit, idle members included. The Crew tab shows the same.
- **Change** ([07-list-ios.png](ux-phone/07-list-ios.png)):
  - The strip shows only members with a job running or a question waiting, as compact chips ("● are you · asking"). It is hidden when nobody is active.
  - Org and Add stay on the Crew tab.
  - Tapping a chip opens that member's thread, as today.
- **Who does this:** story rails that show only what's new (Instagram, Slack's huddles bar), and Linear's "only what needs you" inbox.

## 13. Shell: keys on the keyboard, no tab bar, End shell in ⋯ — Low · S

- **Today** ([today-ios-shell.png](ux-phone/today-ios-shell.png)):
  - The key row (Esc, ^C, Tab, ⇧Tab, Ctrl, ←, ↑…) is fixed above "zsh · End shell", above the tab bar, so it sits far from the keyboard.
  - The prompt shows tofu boxes: the powerline glyphs are missing from the font.
- **Change** ([10-shell.png](ux-phone/10-shell.png)):
  - The key row becomes an input accessory that rides the keyboard: esc, ctrl, ⇥, ^C and the arrows, scrolling for more.
  - The tab bar is hidden (2). End shell moves to ⋯.
  - The terminal font stack gains a Nerd/powerline fallback.
- **Who does this:** Termius (an add-on directly above the system keyboard), Prompt's extra keyboard row, and Blink's Smart Keys.

## 14. Errors and empty states in phone words — Low · S

- **Today:**
  - Changes shows "No changes yet" while a toast says `no such folder: /Users/mokh/.wisp-dev/worktrees/wisp-01a1eb…` over it ([today-ios-changes.png](ux-phone/today-ios-changes.png)). It is a raw path that wraps onto three lines, near the thumb zone.
  - Thread's empty state sets a 30-word sentence in title weight ([today-ios-thread.png](ux-phone/today-ios-thread.png)).
- **Change:**
  - Changes: one empty state that says why ("This task's folder was cleaned up · Restore it") and no toast.
  - Thread: the title "No notes yet", with the explanation as body text.
- **Who sets this:** the HIG and M3 empty-state guidance (a short title, then body and action), and wisp's own design/copy.md.

---

### What's already right (keep)
- **Android:** the extended FAB "New task", M3 navigation bar, top app bar with back arrow, and M3 menu styling.
- **iOS:** the large title, Compose in the nav bar, the nav-bar Back with the edge swipe, and the Thread bottom sheet with a grabber.
- Swipe and long-press on list rows are wired. Making them the main way to act on a row (11) lets the header lose buttons.

### Who does what (suggested)
- **Kit (`packages/ui` phone variants):** 4, 7, the sheet with detents and tabs (3), and the context menu and bottom-sheet menu (6, 8).
- **shell-composer:** 1, 5, 9.
- **shell-task:** 2, 3, 6, 8, 13, 14.
- **shell-list:** 10, 11. **crew:** 12.

Per D-0056, the owner picks from these mocks before anything is built.
