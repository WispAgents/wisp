# Factories in the app: the picker row and the stations band (2026-11-20, ADR-0114)

Owner-list row 65. Rule D-0056 asks for mocks before a redesign. This is a new view, not a redesign: it keeps v1's
controls (the harness picker's one pill and two-pane popover, the task page's band slot that the goal band uses, and
the question card under the turns). The shots below are of the built UI, taken on a scratch wisp with the fake
harnesses and the fake gh. They show one run through the built-in `default` factory: the spec approved, the build,
a failing check sending it back as round 2, the review approving, and the PR merged.

## What it adds

- **New task's picker.** The left list ends with **Factory** ("Spec, build, checks, review, ship"). Its pane lists the
  factories by name: `default` first (Built in · its stations), then the declared ones (their description). The note
  links to the concept page. Picking one makes the pill say **Factory · default**. The launch sends `factory` with no
  harness of its own. The pick is never kept as New task's harness (`runtime.launch`), so a CLI task with no harness
  never lands on a factory.
- **The band** over the run's task (`shell.task:band`, `data-part="factory.band"`):
  - The header: *Factory · default*, where it is (`Build · round 2 of 3`, *Waiting on you*, *Done*, *Failed*,
    *Stopped*) in the run's tone, and a ⋯ menu: Open each station's task, Open the PR, Stop the run.
  - Each station is a step (the kit's `Steps`): done, current, pending, skipped or failed. Its detail is what the
    station gave (the spec's first lines, `1 of 1 passed`, `1 of 1 approve`, the PR), why it failed (the command and
    its last lines), or the question it waits on.
  - Phone and desktop use the same band.
- **The question card** is the run's task's own (`runtime.ask`): *Approve* / *Stop* at the spec, *Merge* / *Leave it
  open* at ship, *One more round* / *Stop* past the limit. Typed words answer it too.
- **TUI and CLI**: `default: ✓ spec · ● build 2/3 · ○ checks · ○ review · ○ ship` in the task header and in `wisp show`.
