Get the app

Wisp Agents on your desk and in your pocket

The desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux is ready today. The phone apps for iPhone and Android are next: join the waitlist and we email you once, when yours is out.

Desktop app

A native window (Tauri, not Electron). Every task, your shortcuts, notifications when an agent needs you, and updates that install themselves.

macOSApple silicon (.dmg) · Intel (.dmg)
Windowsx64 installer · Arm installer
Linux.deb x64 · .deb Arm · AppImage x64 · AppImage Arm

Or with Homebrew: brew install --cask wispagents/tap/wisp. Checksums are in SHA256SUMS.txt on the latest release. The builds aren’t signed yet: on macOS, after the first open, System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway; on Windows, SmartScreen → More info → Run anyway.

Phone apps Waitlist

Built for each phone: Material on Android, Apple’s look on iPhone. Your tasks and crew, the answers and their diffs, a reply box, notifications, and your computers over SSH or Wisp Agents Cloud.

One email when the app is out, nothing else. What we do with your address.

Can’t wait? Open your Wisp Agents Cloud instance, or a computer you put on the Grid, in your phone’s browser and choose Add to Home Screen: it runs full screen. How.

Prefer the terminal?

The wisp command runs everything the app does, and wisp tui is a full terminal UI. It needs Node.js 18 or later.

A command just failed? Type wisp "check the error above": the agent gets the command, its exit code, its output and your folder, with secrets masked. How it works.
